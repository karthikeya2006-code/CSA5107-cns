ABC Technologies
Employee Leave Policy - 2026

Annual Leave

Employees are entitled to 18 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Casual Leave

Employees are entitled to 12 days of casual leave per calendar year.

Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 10 days of sick leave per calendar year.

Leave requests should normally be submitted through the employee portal at least two working days before the planned leave.

Emergency leave may be requested directly from the reporting manager.
